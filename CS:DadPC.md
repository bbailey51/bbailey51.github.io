#PC Issues
### Cleaning Incident
One day I was asked by Dad, to take a look at cleaning the PC, I think he was concerned about temps potentially, I think a weird error occurred though can't attribute it solely to temps however I knew he had the PC for over 2 years and hasn't been cleaned yet.  Looking at it, it really really needed it badly.  I did get a bunch of dust out of it though it needed more extensive cleaning which didn't have the time for.  Took all the precautions of static electricity prevention map and a wrist band to ground everything, didn't use a vacuum, or do anything to wild to take it apart, though when getting it hooked back up, the video output on the TV was incomprehensibly smeared.  I don't remember the exact wording my dad put it from his analog TV experience, but best I can interpret was the VSync was way out of synce and not outputting to the correct way.  The UEFI screen and MSI splash screen was unreadable and couldn't try to navigate blindly what is going on.  I think a few times boot up would be a black screen as well.  I couldn't figure it out for the life of me and I was out of time for the day and felt horrible that I may have broken the PC.  Made the decision that I'll take it home and deep dive some more.  The whole drive back I couldn't shake the thought of just waiting 30 minutes and trying again.  I was going to work on it next day, but just wanted to know if it would boot up, so made the whole dining room area my PC lab, got it all connected up, and with a monitor and low and behold the login screen for MSI logo & Windows 11 comes up unsmeared.  So crisis averted, and can focus next day on a complete clean up of the internals and fully update everything from BIOS to driver updates for everything.  Took a few hours to clean due to so much of the dust being compacted on the fans, radiators and so many other places.  Took a long time brushing, cleaning, using canned air to get the PC to a respectable state.  Got everything all put back, booted the PC to make sure it was good to go, and get all the updates setup.  Last test was to open up iRacing (our main racing game sim we both use) and make sure that was up and running.  It was all good, next day drove back down to my father's place, set everything back up, and was all good to go.  One thing that may have contributed to the original issue after cleaning is I noticed originally my Dad put the HDMI cord into the motherboard HDMI port instead of using the GPU he has so make sense to make that switch when putting it back and may have thrown the PC for a loop.  Told him and showed him the issue so he can use his PC to it's fullest

### Unable to Boot Pass UEFI
Situation occurred when my Dad after doing an update on his PC disconnected it and moved it to another room to prevent loud noises potentially with a new roommate, but when he got it all connected, at the MSI splash screen for the UEFI would freeze.  This was happening during his Pedal Issues (story below) so was another thing I need to investigate when I get his Sim Rig set back up again.  With no getting past the UEFI screen, I can't remote into his PC to see what's going on and went through the basic steps of rebooting and no issue, and waiting 30 minutes off any power and rebooting and still nothing.  So I would need to go over there to take a look.  Turned it on I see the issue, next step was I went to Windows Recovery Environment and just do a quick system restore back prior to the issue occurring since it happened after an update.  While getting his rig back up and setup, system restore completed however the on boot up another freeze after the MSI splash screen.  I gave it a few minutes just to see if it'll get past but it didn't.  My next step which should have been the first thing I did was disconnecting USB connections just to see if the issue was stemming to something connecting to it.  While taking a look at the back of the PC for anything connected, I see the HDMI cord is once again plugged in the wrong port on the motherboard and not the GPU.  My father was helping hold the PC up as I was making the connection switch but he accidentally shut the PC down and panicked, I was still chill and connected the HDMI port to the GPU and rebooted, and the PC booted up fully.  Once again the issue was video output getting confused where it was connected to but instead of vertical synce going weird, it was a full on crash before Windows boot.  Again had to reiterate to dad put the HDMI cord only in the GPU ports and not the Motherboard and showing where.

### Pedal Issues
This issue occurred when my father was one day racing and noticing that his throttle input was very delayed and not corresponding what he was actually doing on the throttle and brakes.  I had him remote me in via Quick Assist to see what the pedals inputs were and had him go full full throttle and the throttle input was super delayed and shaky from where he was 100% on the throttle and same thing on the brakes.  We tried updating drivers on the podium, updating the program that manages the Sim Rig components, however nothing was working.  I escalated into a ticket to get some ideas or having to get this fixed.  Did get steps for a clean uninstall and reinstall which a few days later completed and still having the same issue.  Only thing I can do just to confirm this is either a rig issue and have to send a part of it to fix it or a PC issue which there wasn't anything crucial to keep so a clean install was on the table to do.  So I decided take it to my place and set it up with my PC and see if it replicates the issue.  Took it home connected it and everything, but the throttle still had a similar issue with the throttle input being not quite right where fully depressed it's shaking.  I was going to do some cleaning in the throttle to see if debris or something is going on, though low and behold I get a deal to upgrade my own pedals thereby giving my old working pedals to my father as the fix.  When I came back to my dad's place to firstly get his PC to not freeze at the UEFI screen, and then get his sim rig set back up, everything worked perfectly.  After playing around with my old pedals to get it calibrated for his PC and get it setup where it's comfortable for him and have iRacing get calibrated to it, everything worked perfectly.  Additionally gave him a few more pointers on getting controls to his liking and no sense of pedals acting weird with the PC so believe the old pedals just had something weird going on and calibration to it was making it do weird things.
